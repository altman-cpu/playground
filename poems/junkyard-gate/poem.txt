c cure

if it had to be a junkyard
let it have a gate.
outside the gate had to be the world,

he had to be the only one
who didn't know what he owned —
more than the world could keep,

if he just wanted it that way
instead of his inner thoughts,
which weren't even in his best interest,
or whoever's best interest
his head had.

so much crystal to smoke
so much not even fennel.
