field notes

These are invitations, not instructions or the only meanings. Take what helps; leave the rest.

i — c cure

Think about what is yours to protect, and what the gate keeps out as well as in. Act by naming one boundary you can keep without shutting out help. Express what is valuable to you without making ownership the whole story.

ii — e12

Think about what gets easier through repetition, and what gets lost in the process. Act by checking who benefits before you automate or repeat a task. Express the unfinished thought; it is allowed to remain unfinished.

iii — void

Think about the distance between wanting, asking, and being heard. Act by asking clearly and respecting the answer. Express the want without treating another person as if they owe it to you.

iv — genesis

Think about whose work makes the money and who gets to decide where it goes. Act with clear agreements, fair pay, and room for anyone to say no. Express the joy of making something together without confusing money with consent or worth.

v — this world

Think about what makes a place worth returning to. Act in a way that makes the next visit easier for someone else, too. Express the reason quietly or loudly, as it needs to be — care need not perform to be real.
